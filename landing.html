How it works
1

Stock the shelf

Drop in PDFs, Word files, notes. Or just type the rules it has to follow. Every class gets its own shelf and they never mix.

2

Students ask

They type a question the way they’d text a friend. The answer comes back out of your shelf, tagged with what it was pulled from.

3

Read the gaps

Questions sort themselves by topic, and anything your material couldn’t answer gets flagged. It quietly turns into your list of what to teach next.
